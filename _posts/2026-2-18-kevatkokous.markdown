Hi all Bikepolians!

Welcome to the Bikepoli’s Spring General Meeting, which will take place on Wednesday 25.02.2026 at 17:00. The official venue will be Huoltopaja (Otakaari 20, Espoo), and members may also participate in conversation remotely if they wish. However, one needs to be present in order to cast a vote.

The agenda of the meeting includes the presentation of the statutory matters, such as the action report and financial statements for the past year and discharge from liability, as well as the budget and action plan for the current year.
